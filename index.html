How One Win Works

✨ One Win helps you focus on the single most important task you can complete today.

📝 How it works

• Add tasks using the blue + button.

• Each task has a deadline date, time, and urgency level.

• All tasks are stored in your Brain Dump.

⭐ Your One Win

The app automatically selects one task for you to focus on:

• By deadline (soonest first), or

• By urgency (Very Urgent → Urgent → Not So Urgent).

You can change this in Settings.

✔️ Completing tasks

When you tick a task as Complete on the One Win screen, it is removed from your Brain Dump and the next task appears automatically.

🧠 Brain Dump

Tap the pink brain to view all your tasks. You can hide or show them, scroll through them one at a time, and delete any task you no longer need.

🔔 Reminders

You can set a reminder interval in Settings. The app will gently remind you about your current One Win at the interval you choose.

👤 Your data

Your tasks stay on your device and are linked to your unique User ID.
